Huurwoningen in Rotterdam

Are you looking for a rental property in Rotterdam? At vb&t Verhuurmakelaars, you’ll find a wide range of rental properties in Rotterdam and the surrounding area. From modern flats in the city centre to spacious family homes in leafy residential areas. As specialists in rental properties, vb&t Verhuurmakelaars help house hunters find a suitable rental property in Rotterdam every day.

Rotterdam is the second-largest city in the Netherlands and is known worldwide for its impressive skyline, modern architecture and international character. With Europe’s largest port, excellent amenities and a vibrant city life, Rotterdam is an attractive place to live for first-time renters, young professionals, families, expats and senior citizens.

Why live in Rotterdam?

Rotterdam is a city that is constantly evolving. Whilst other Dutch cities are known for their historic centres, Rotterdam stands out for its modern architecture, innovative projects and dynamic atmosphere.

The city offers a unique combination of living, working, studying and leisure. Whether you opt for a flat in the city centre, a property by the water or a family home in a quiet neighbourhood: Rotterdam has a suitable neighbourhood to suit every housing need.

At vb&t Verhuurmakelaars, you’ll find rental properties in various parts of Rotterdam, so there’s always a home available to suit your situation.

Architecture, culture and famous landmarks

Rotterdam is known as the architectural capital of the Netherlands. World-famous landmarks such as the Erasmus Bridge, the Markthal, the Cube Houses and De Rotterdam define the modern cityscape.

Rotterdam also has a lot to offer culturally. Visit the Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen Depot, the Kunsthal Rotterdam, the Wereldmuseum, or enjoy a performance at Theater Rotterdam or De Doelen.

In addition, the Oude Haven attracts many visitors every year thanks to its cosy terraces, historic ships and charming surroundings. Anyone renting a property in Rotterdam lives in a city where culture, architecture and innovation come together.

Shopping, dining and nightlife

Rotterdam offers endless possibilities for lovers of shopping, dining and entertainment. From the Koopgoot and the Lijnbaan to the many boutiques, food halls and specialist shops scattered throughout the city.

Rotterdam also has an excellent reputation for its cuisine. Thanks to its multicultural population, you’ll find restaurants serving food from all corners of the globe. Popular areas such as Witte de Withstraat, Katendrecht, the Markthal and the Oude Haven offer plenty of opportunities to enjoy food, drink and a night out.

Moreover, its vibrant nightlife makes Rotterdam a magnet for students, young professionals and city lovers.

Green living in the port city

Despite its urban character, Rotterdam offers a surprising amount of green space. The Kralingse Bos and the Kralingse Plas are among the city’s most popular recreational areas. In addition, Het Park near the Euromast, the Vroesenpark and the Zuiderpark offer plenty of space for walking, exercising or relaxing.

This means that in Rotterdam, you can combine the convenience of a global city with plenty of opportunities to unwind in nature.

International and diverse

With Europe’s largest port, international companies, universities and colleges, Rotterdam has a strong international character. The city attracts thousands of students, expats and professionals from home and abroad every year.

Thanks to the diversity of its residents, cultures and amenities, Rotterdam is one of the most multicultural cities in the Netherlands. This is reflected in the streetscape, the hospitality sector, events and the wide range of housing on offer.

Easily accessible by car and public transport

Rotterdam is extremely well connected. Via the A13, A15, A16, A20 and A29 motorways, you can easily travel to cities such as The Hague, Utrecht, Breda and Amsterdam.

Public transport is also very well organised. Rotterdam Central Station is one of the most important stations in the Netherlands and offers fast train connections throughout the country. In addition, metros, trams, buses and water taxis provide excellent connections within the city.

Rotterdam Blaak station is right in the city centre and offers direct access to popular attractions such as the Markthal, the Cube Houses and the Oude Haven.
