About Vesteda

Vesteda is a Dutch residential investor that focuses primarily on the mid-rental segment. Vesteda invests funds for institutional investors, such as pension funds and insurers. Vesteda’s homes are mainly located in economically strong regions and core urban regions. Search on our website for suitable housing and register for free!
